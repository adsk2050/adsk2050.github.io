Latest project

Shrota: AI phone surveys

Shrota is an AI-powered CATI (computer-assisted telephone interviewing) platform for running phone surveys in India, in Hindi and other Indian languages. See it live at getaicati.com.

Surveys are designed in a web builder and compiled into LangGraph conversation graphs. An AI interviewer then runs them over real phone calls through LiveKit and SIP.

Studies dial contact lists in waves, with call records, live monitoring and per-call tracing.

Technologies used:

  • LangGraph
  • LiveKit
  • SIP
  • Python
  • React
  • PostgreSQL
  • Langfuse

Summer school

3D Vision Summer School 2024

Attended the 3D Vision Summer School (3DVSS) at IIIT Bangalore, co-organised with IIIT Hyderabad, from 26 May to 1 June 2024. Seven days of lectures and hands-on labs, one theme a day: 2D foundations, geometry processing, deep learning on 3D data, rendering and NeRF, 3D humans, Gaussian Splatting, and generative 3D.

Worked through the labs with Open3D, Trimesh and PyMeshLab, PointNet and MeshCNN, NeRF (instant-NGP), SMPL-X, 4D Humans and PIFu for human reconstruction, 3D Gaussian Splatting, and Point-E, Shap-E and TripoSR for text-to-3D and image-to-3D.

The image is my own re-run of the geometry processing lab: one RGB-D photo lifted into a coloured 3D point cloud with Open3D.

Technologies used:

  • PyTorch
  • Open3D
  • PointNet
  • NeRF
  • SMPL-X
  • Gaussian Splatting
  • Shap-E

ML project

Personalized Recommendations at Scale

Built as part of the Co:Rise program in July 2022.

Developed a two-tower candidate generation model and a multi-task re-ranking model for personalized recommendations at scale.

Implemented a contextual multi-armed bandit model with epsilon-greedy policy for exploration-exploitation trade-offs.

Technologies used:

  • TensorFlow
  • Python
  • Multi-armed Bandits
  • Two-tower Models
