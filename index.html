AI/ML Engineer

I have been building AI/ML systems for past 6 years, and I am an IIT Guwahati alumnus. These days I mostly build LLM systems end to end - multi-agent systems, RAG pipelines, voice agents, and the evaluation and guardrails around them.

Right now I am building Shrota on my own. It is an AI phone-survey platform: a researcher designs a survey, and a voice agent calls each respondent and asks the questions in Hindi. It is pre-launch and runs on test calls.

Before this I was a Machine Learning Engineer at Coreworks AI, where I owned the correctness of agent output on a product that turns business data into reports, decks and spreadsheet models. Before that I built voice agents, RAG and multi-agent systems at Aidora, a legal knowledge graph at Staram Analytics, document AI at Insurance Samadhan, and recommender systems at Tata Digital, where I was promoted after one year.

Apart from that I run my own research. Right now it is about getting an LLM to rewrite only the part of a structured output that changed, instead of all of it.
