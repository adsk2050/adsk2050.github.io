About me

I graduated from IIT Guwahati in 2020 with a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering and a Minor in Product Design. I got interested in computer science early in college and never looked back.

For past 6 years I have built all kind of AI/ML systems, starting from recommender systems and computer vision all the way to NLP, document AI and LLM agents these days. I have worked at a large company like Tata Digital and at small startups, across e-commerce, insurance, legal tech and conversational AI.

What I am working on now

I am building Shrota, an AI phone-survey platform, on my own. Apart from that I run my own research on structured outputs: can an LLM write only the part of a schema-bound output that changed, and splice it back with the schema still valid? In my experiments on Gemini, edits took 172x fewer output tokens. The code is on GitHub.

I am also studying speech processing. I wrote up what I learned about text-to-speech in a post you can listen to.
